# Reference Top Patterns

The comparator scores your current components against four reference top patterns.
They are pattern archetypes with heuristic component estimates. They are not measured on Indian data.
Treat the match as a rough analogy, never as a forecast. Read `point_in_time_evaluation.md` before any calibration claim.

## Blow-off bubble top

- Speculative excess and very low fear
- Heavy distribution for weeks, and leaders already broken
- Breadth diverged for a long time before the index peaked
- Typical estimates: Distribution 90, Leading 85, Defensive 70, Breadth 95, Technical 60, Sentiment 90
- Outcome class: very large decline over months to years

## Slow financial-crisis top

- Leading sectors (for example credit-sensitive groups) peak well before the index
- Defensive rotation builds for months
- The composite sits in Orange for months before Red
- Typical estimates: Distribution 80, Leading 75, Defensive 85, Breadth 80, Technical 70, Sentiment 75
- Lesson: patience in reducing exposure beats a single binary call

## Fast sell-off top

- A sharp 10-20% decline in a few months, then a quick V-shaped recovery
- Moderate distribution, mixed leadership, little defensive rotation
- Typical estimates: Distribution 60, Leading 50, Defensive 40, Breadth 55, Technical 65, Sentiment 55
- Lesson: Orange-zone readings often end in a correction, not a bear market

## Rate-hike growth-led top

- Growth and speculative leaders peak many months before the index
- Heavy distribution, a broken index structure, mixed sentiment
- Typical estimates: Distribution 75, Leading 90, Defensive 60, Breadth 70, Technical 80, Sentiment 65
- Lesson: leading-stock health is the earliest warning in this pattern

## Common Themes

1. Severity ranges from a 10-20% correction to a decline of 40% or more.
2. Speed ranges from three months to several years.
3. Leaders usually weaken before the index.
4. Breadth divergence can lead the index top by months.

## How the zones read

- Orange (41-60): a correction-type reading (10-20%)
- Red (61-80): a major-decline reading (20-35%)
- Critical (81-100): a bubble-top reading (40%+)

These ranges are retrospective illustrations, not measured outcome frequencies.
